Welcome! Set up your tracker in three steps:
  1. Team rotation (on a phone: Settings tab at the bottom): add your team (A, B, C or D) and the date you joined it. Your shifts then appear on the calendar, going back to earlier dates too.
  2. Balances & entitlements (on a phone: Balances tab): enter your annual leave, brought-forward days and OIL as of a date (the starting point).
  3. Settings (on a phone: Settings tab): add your birthday and check the default quotas.
Everything is saved in this browser only. Use Export JSON to back up or move to another device, and Import JSON to restore.
